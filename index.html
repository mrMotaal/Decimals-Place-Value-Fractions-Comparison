📌 Shifting Digits in a Place-Value Chart ($\times 10$ and $\times \frac{1}{10}$)

2Find $6.347 \times 10$ and $1.59 \times \dfrac{1}{10}$.
3Pacific Ocean Salinity: The average salinity in the Pacific Ocean is $35$ parts per thousand. Lucas writes $35 = 3.5 \times 100$. Is his answer correct? Explain.
✍️ Student Workspace (20 Lines)
✅ Model Step-by-Step Solution Question 8
  • 1) Place-Value Rule in $22.222$: A digit in one place represents $10$ times as much as it represents in the place to its right, and $\dfrac{1}{10}$ of what it represents in the place to its left (e.g., $2\text{ tenths} \times 10 = 2\text{ ones}$, and $2\text{ tenths} \times \dfrac{1}{10} = 2\text{ hundredths}$).
  • 2) Shifting Digits: $6.347 \times 10 = 63.470$ (or $63.47$), and $1.59 \times \dfrac{1}{10} = 0.159$.
  • 3) Lucas's Statement ($35 = 3.5 \times 100$): Lucas is incorrect. The decimal point in $3.5$ moves one place to the right to become $35$, so $3.5$ is multiplied by $10$ ($35 = 3.5 \times 10$), not $100$.
$6.347 \times 10 = 63.47$,   $1.59 \times \dfrac{1}{10} = 0.159$,   $35 = 3.5 \times 10$
